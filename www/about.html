Daily Tracking - Goalie and Miler Lite

Daily Tracking - Goalie and Miler Lite is two small web apps that log daily entries to your own Google Sheet: Goalie for daily goals and habits, and Miler Lite for business mileage. They're quick phone-friendly forms, so you don't have to open a spreadsheet on a small screen.

Goalie

Goalie tracks daily goals. The questions come from a settings tab in your spreadsheet, and can be choices, checkboxes, numbers or text. Each day you open Goalie, answer the questions and click Submit, and it adds one row to the spreadsheet with the date and your answers. Click Edit to rename the spreadsheet, or to add, rename, reorder or remove questions.

Miler Lite

Miler Lite logs drives for mileage reimbursement or taxes. Pick a business, tap a saved trip or type the purpose and destination, and enter the odometer readings. It works out the miles and adds a row to that year's mileage log in your spreadsheet, where the reimbursement is calculated. Click Edit to rename the spreadsheet, change the rate per mile, or add, rename, reorder or remove businesses and saved trips.

How the apps use your Google account

You sign in with Google and allow access to only the Google Drive files you use with the apps. Click New to create a starter tracker spreadsheet in your Drive, or Open to choose one you already have in Google's file picker. The apps then read that spreadsheet's settings tab to build the form, and add a row each time you submit. They can't see the rest of your Drive. They run entirely in your browser. There is no server, and your entries go straight from your browser to your spreadsheet. See the privacy policy for details.

Who it's for

The apps are a free personal project by Andrew Macheret. All you need is a Google account. Each browser remembers the spreadsheets you've used, so you can switch between them, like one per year. The source code is public in the goalie and miler-lite repos.
