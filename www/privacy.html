Privacy policy

For Goalie and Miler Lite, which sign in with Google as the Daily Tracking - Goalie and Miler Lite app. Last updated November 24, 2026.

The short version

Both apps are web pages that read and write your own Google Sheet, straight from your browser. There is no server or database behind them. Your data is not collected, stored, sold or shared by the developer.

What the apps do with your Google account

When you click Authorize, Google asks you to let the app see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with it. The apps use that access only to:

  • create a starter tracker spreadsheet in your Google Drive when you click New
  • read the settings tab of the tracker spreadsheet you picked or created, to build the form
  • add one row to that spreadsheet each time you click Submit
  • change the spreadsheet's name and questions when you click Edit and Save in Goalie
  • move the spreadsheet to your Google Drive trash, only if you click Remove and choose Move to trash

Google only lets the apps reach spreadsheets you chose in Google's file picker or that the app created for you. They can't see the rest of your Google Drive, and they don't see your Google password. Google gives the page an access token that expires after an hour. The page keeps it in your browser's local storage until then, so reloading doesn't ask you to sign in again. It's only sent to Google, and Sign Out deletes it.

You can remove the apps' access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections.

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the apps store

Each app saves a few things in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • the Google access token, until it expires an hour after you sign in
  • the ID and name of the spreadsheets you've used with it, so it can open the right one next time
  • a copy of each spreadsheet's settings, so the form shows up faster. For Goalie that's the questions and their choices. For Miler Lite it's the businesses, saved trips and latest odometer reading.

Clearing your browser's site data for andrewmacheret.com deletes it.

Analytics

The pages use Google Analytics to count visits. It sets cookies and records the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, and your rough location from your IP address. It doesn't get anything from your Google account or your spreadsheet, or anything you type into the apps. The developer only sees totals, like visits per page. You can block it with an ad blocker or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

What the apps do not do

  • No ads
  • Your spreadsheet data is never sent to the developer or anyone besides Google
  • No data is used for advertising or to train AI models

Other services

The pages are hosted on Amazon Web Services, which keeps standard access logs (IP address, page address, browser type and time) for 30 days. The pages also load Google's sign-in and file picker scripts from Google. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The apps' source code is public in the goalie and miler-lite repos, so you can check what they do.
